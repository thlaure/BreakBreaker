# BreakBreaker — Specifications: Acceptance

> **Purpose of this file:** validation and acceptance cases (RC), gap with the current prototype.  
> Part of the BreakBreaker specifications (version 1.0.1), split into several files. Back to the [specifications index](SPECS%20-%20Index.md) (overall table of contents, version history, conventions, file map).

## 21. Validation and acceptance criteria

### 21.1 Evidence

Rule tests; integration with a controlled clock; browser with rendering; real phone; hands-on onboarding trials. No test of the old 3D engine validates the rendering or the inputs of the future 2D game.

### 21.3 Visual acceptance

Assess the real on-screen size: Paul identifiable thanks to his light accent, weapons on the ground easy to spot, peaceful groups distinct, flags readable, stones in flight/on the ground different, heals and toolboxes distinct, victim identifiable, health bar and alert readable, strike and throw-back understandable. Check foot and building depth, crisp pixel art, no isometric camera.

### 21.4 Players and performance

Ten-minute scenario on target devices, then thirty minutes of continuous session. First panel of five to eight people: understanding of the protected groups, the stone stock, dodging and throwing back without lengthy verbal explanation.

The first minute must teach movement and a recoverable throw. A first rescue then introduces the emergency choice; the two simultaneous events are added only after this learning. Check that the touch interface does not ask to move, turn the camera and aim at the same time: the camera is automatic.
