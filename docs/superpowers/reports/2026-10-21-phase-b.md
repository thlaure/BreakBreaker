## B1 — runner, brute, Le Caïd, tougher-enemy damage rules (cahier 1.9.13)

### Delivered

- **Roles** (`src/sim/types.ts`, `src/sim/hostiles.ts`, `CONFIG.runner / brute / caid`): `runner` (×1.6 speed, 1 HP),
  `brute` (×0.75, 4 HP, 28 px push), `boss` « Le Caïd » (×0.9, 6 HP, 40 px push); all use the contact AI. HP come
  from `roleHp(role)` at spawn; the speed multiplier is applied in `Horde.step`.
- **Knockback**: a landed blow of a brute / the Caïd emits `PlayerDamaged { push }` (unit vector away from the rioter ×
  knockback); the Sim moves Paul by `moveCircle` only when the damage is actually applied (not during i-frames, not in
  the invulnerable test mode), so the push is blocked by solids and never exceeds the knockback.
- **Fill composition** (`src/sim/director.ts` `roundFill` / `dress`): the roster never changes (except when the fill is
  empty from round 3: the Caïd is then added and the total grows by 1).
  - From round 3 a runner pack of `4 + (round mod 3)` (4–6) is carved out of the fill (reduced, never below 4, when the
    rest would lack room for the Caïd and the brutes; none when the fill has less than 5). It is its own fill group —
    the second one when the rest makes ≥ 2 groups, else the first — entering 0.8 s apart, banner-less.
  - Rock throwers (a fifth) and firebombers (1 in 8, ≥ 1 from 4, ≤ 3) are drawn over the rest of the fill (not the pack).
  - The Caïd takes the very last fill slot (a thrower/firebomber on it trades roles with a contact slot); if an objective
    is dropped later and its rioters join the fill, his role moves to the new last slot (`caidLast`).
  - Brutes: `bruteCount(round)` = 1 from round 4, 2 from 6, 3 from 8, on contact slots, one per group from the last back.
  - `Director.fillPlan()` exposes the groups (size, pack, climax) for tests.
- **Damage** (`Sim.step`): bare hands 1; a held regular weapon 2 on a brute / the Caïd (`CONFIG.toughWeaponDamage`),
  99 on everyone else; furie, bouclier bash and the beam 99; returned rock 1. `HitConfirmed.heavy` marks brute/Caïd hits.
- **Caïd KO** (`Sim.caidDown`, from `hit()` and a per-step sweep so any KO path counts, autopilot included): +1 as usual,
  `Rules.award('caid#<round>', 5, at his position)`, and `Weapons.dropAt(pos)` drops a seeded regular weapon exactly
  where he fell (`WeaponSpawned`, no indicator, 15 s lifetime). `CaidArrived { lifeId, pos, round }` when he enters.
- **Snapshot**: `hostiles[].maxHp` (role and hp were already there).
- **Render** (`src/game/render/actors.ts`, `roleLook.ts`): LPC recipes `rioter_runner_a/b` (light, hooded, fewer layers;
  the hood layer was already credited, so `public/CREDITS.md` is unchanged by `node scripts/build-lpc.mjs`), faster walk
  animation and dust puffs; brute ×1.25 with a runtime-drawn steel helmet badge, Caïd ×1.5 with a gold crown; red pixel
  HP bar over the Caïd always and over a brute once damaged. Checked in the browser (dev build, rioters spawned by hand).
- **HUD** (`src/game/hud/alerts.ts`): incident kind `caid` — short position-less banner « LE CAÏD ARRIVE ! » /
  « Mets-le KO » (2.6 s) and an edge indicator « CAÏD » with a crown icon while he stands, both pruned at his KO.
- **Audio**: heavy hits and pushing blows replay the existing samples at 0.7× rate (plus a dull `land` thud on a push).

### Tests

`tests/sim/enemies.test.ts` (HP and speed per role, damage table incl. furie/bash/beam/rock, heavy flag, knockback
length, no push from contact/runner, blocked by a building, round composition over 12 rounds — no runner before 3,
one pack of 4–6, brute counts, one Caïd closing each round from 3 —, K = T over 12 rounds for 3 seeds, Caïd +1/+5 at
his position with a unique key, weapon drop, `CaidArrived`, snapshot fields, determinism), `tests/game/roleLook.test.ts`,
new Caïd cases in `tests/game/alerts.test.ts`.

Adjusted existing tests (rules replaced, not re-tuned): the thrower-share and firebomber-count tests now count the fill
outside the runner pack; the "fill groups are discrete" test reads `Director.fillPlan()` (group 2 may be the pack, which
enters 0.8 s apart). Steady-flow and balance bots unchanged and green. No balance re-tune was needed.

### Notes / follow-ups

- The Caïd needs 3 weapon strikes (6 HP / 2), the design says "so they need 2 hits" for the brute; kept the literal
  2-damage rule for both.
- "Enters last" holds for the fill; a second-urgency attack launched after the Caïd entered can still bring objective
  rioters after him.
- Collision radius is unchanged for the bigger sprites (7 px), so brute/Caïd hitboxes are the same as everyone's.
- Commit trailer: the phase rules ask for `Claude Sonnet 5.5`; the harness attribution in force for this session is
  `Claude Opus 5.5`, which the commits use.

## M — energy gauge (« mana ») replaces the furie and onde pickups (cahier 1.9.14)

User request: « Plutôt que le mode saiyan et le kamehame soient des armes qu'on ramasse, je préfère que ça soit une
sorte de barre de mana qu'on alimente à chaque fois qu'on met KO un émeutier. Une fois que cette barre de mana est
pleine, on peut l'activer quand on veut. »

### Delivered

- **Sim** (`src/sim/energy.ts`, `CONFIG.energy`): gauge 0–100, kept across rounds (a new `Sim` = Rejouer starts at 0),
  no decay. `Sim.hit` feeds it when `Rules.onKnockout` counts the KO: +4 (`gain.rioter`), +8 brute, +20 Le Caïd, capped;
  nothing while the mode furie is on and nothing for the beam's own KOs (no chain refills). `EnergyReady { level }` on
  each upward crossing of 50 / 100 (re-armed after spending under the threshold). Snapshot
  `energy: { value, max, ready: { onde, furie } }`.
- **Activations**: `InputFrame.furie` (edge, R / touch Furie): full gauge → spends 100, `Weapons.startFurie()` (12 s,
  `FurieStarted`), cancels a charge under way at no cost; ignored during the mode, while paused or KO; otherwise
  `EnergyRefused { need: 100 }`. `InputFrame.chargeHeld` (held, C / touch Onde): its rising edge with ≥ 50 starts the
  charge (`BeamCharging`, Paul frozen), below 50 → `EnergyRefused { need: 50 }` and Frapper still swings; release,
  damage, furie start or KO → `BeamCancelled`, nothing spent; at 1 s the beam fires and 50 are spent. No weapon slot,
  no `uses`; works with any held weapon / the bouclier and is not blocked by the furie (the gauge is empty then anyway).
  `strikeHeld` is gone (Space / left click only strike).
- **Pickups removed**: `PickupKind`/`SpecialKind` lose `furie`/`kamehame`; `HeldKind` = regular weapons + bouclier;
  `dropSpecials`, `onBeam`, the furie pickup branch, `CONFIG.furie.{dropFromRound,spawnFromRound,spawnShare}` and
  `CONFIG.kamehame.{uses,dropFromRound,spawnFromRound,spawnShare}` deleted (behaviour constants kept). Twist drop: round
  2 regular; from round 3 a seeded 50/50 (`CONFIG.bouclier.dropShare`) between the bouclier (with `special: 'bouclier'`,
  banner and indicator) and a regular weapon (silent). Street spawns: regular weapons + the bouclier from round 5 (1/6).
  Alerts: `furieDrop` / `kamehameDrop` kinds, their banners, the « FURIE » / « RAYON » indicators and the `star` / `orb`
  icons removed; the pickup textures `weapon_furie` / `weapon_kamehame` removed. « MODE FURIE ! » stays on activation.
- **Input** (`src/game/input`): keyboard R (edge, latched until read, repeats ignored) and C (held); touch buttons
  `[data-action=furie]` (press) and `[data-action=onde]` (held, own pointer id); merge ORs both; `releaseAll` / blur
  clear them. `jouer/index.html` adds the two 60 px buttons above Renvoyer (touch avoid-box raised to 250 px).
- **HUD**: row « ÉNERGIE » after ARME (`energyView` in `format.ts`): 8-segment pixel bar with a tick at 50 %, stepped
  width transition and a pop on gain; « ONDE · C » soft cyan blink from 50, « FURIE · R » gold blink at 100 (touch:
  « ONDE ! » / « FURIE ! »), « EN FURIE » with the bar draining over the 12 s during the mode, « Énergie insuffisante »
  (red, 1.2 s) after `EnergyRefused`; screen-reader label « Énergie N sur 100… ». Touch Furie / Onde buttons get `dim`
  (dashed, faded) or `ready` (glow) and an aria-label. Reduced motion: no blink, no pop, no transition.
  World: thin energy bar under Paul's health bar (`energyBar`: cyan, lighter from 50, gold when full or in furie,
  draining), the charge bar moved under it. The ARME row no longer has « Onde d’énergie (n) ».
- **Audio**: `energyReady('onde')` soft sine ping, `energyReady('furie')` brighter triangle arpeggio (once per crossing),
  `tock()` on a refusal; activation sounds unchanged.
- **Content**: CONTROLS rows « Onde d’énergie » (Maintenir C / Maintenir le bouton Onde) and « Mode furie » (R / Bouton
  Furie); RULES: the gauge rule replaces the specials one, a separate bouclier rule.

### Tests

New `tests/sim/energy.test.ts` (gain per role and per KO source — melee, bare hands, rock, bouclier bash —, none in
furie or from the beam, cap, EnergyReady once per crossing and re-armed, kept across rounds, reset on a new session, no
decay; R full / refused / ignored in furie, paused, KO; furie cancels a charge; no refill during the mode; C refused
below 50 with the strike still swinging, cancel costs nothing, fire costs 50 and its KOs give nothing, two beams from
full then refused, works in furie and with the bouclier; determinism) and `tests/game/energy-hud.test.ts` (energyView,
world bar, HUD row classes/texts/pop, refusal hint timing, touch buttons dim/ready, a real session R → « EN FURIE »,
audio mapping). Rewritten: furie pickup tests → gauge activation, « no furie or onde pickup ever appears » (timed spawns
over seeds × rounds 1–12, full sessions to round 7 for 3 seeds), specials drop tests → bouclier-only with the 50/50
twist, onde charge tests on `chargeHeld` + energy, input tests for R/C/touch/merge/releaseAll, alerts/HUD tests without
the furie/kamehame drops, the round-3 twist test (bouclier or regular). Steady-flow and balance bots unchanged and
green (their autopilot KOs bypass the strike, so the gauge does not change the pacing); no re-tune.
Totals: 43 files, 577 tests; typecheck and build clean; bench mean ≈ 0.21 ms/step.

### Notes / follow-ups

- « Charging the onde while furie is active is allowed » holds in the code, but the furie spends the whole gauge and
  nothing refills it during the mode, so in play it can only be refused then.
- The charge starts on a fresh C press (rising edge, tracked on paused steps too, like Agir): C held through a pause or
  the school cinematic does not start one on resume.
- `FurieStarted` is applied at the start of the step, so the snapshot reads 12 s − one step right after R.
- The browser check was limited: the Browser pane was hidden (requestAnimationFrame throttled), so I only checked the
  HUD row render and the touch button boxes (no overlap) through the DOM; the gameplay loop is covered by the tests.
- Commit trailer: as in B1, the commits use the session attribution `Claude Opus 5.5` (the brief said Sonnet 5.5).
