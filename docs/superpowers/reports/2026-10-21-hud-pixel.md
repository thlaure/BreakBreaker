# HUD pixel redesign — report (2026-10-21)

Branch `feat/hud-pixel`. User request: health above Paul's head, score as a bigger pixel-font list top-left, toolbox indicator removed. Sim untouched (no new snapshot field).

## What changed

### Health above Paul (world)
- New `src/game/render/healthBar.ts`: `PaulHealthBar` (one Phaser Graphics, depth `DEPTH.overlay + 1`) and the pure `healthColor(hp, max)` (green > 50 %, yellow > 25 %, red otherwise).
- 28×5 px bar: `#14121c` outline, `#3a3448` track, coloured fill (at least 1 px while hp > 0), a 5×5 pixel heart on its left (alpha 0.75). Bottom-centre anchored 46 px above Paul's feet, whole-pixel positions.
- Scale like the victim bubbles: `max(1, round(2 / zoom))` (×2 at zoom 1, ×1 at zoom 2–3).
- On an hp drop: white fill for 120 ms and a ±1 px horizontal shake for 180 ms (no shake under `prefers-reduced-motion`; the flash stays).
- Hidden while Paul is KO; visible during furie (the aura is behind Paul, the bar is above everything around him).
- Wired in `WorldScene.render` right after `PaulView.update`, anchored to Paul's sprite.
- HUD: the visible health row is gone; a visually hidden `.bb-sr` `role="progressbar"` keeps `aria-label` / text « Santé 85 sur 100 » with `aria-valuenow` / `aria-valuemax` (`hpLabel` in format.ts).

### Score list (HUD)
- `.bb-top` now holds the hidden progressbar, a `ul.bb-stats` list, then the incident cards (unchanged, below the list, 14 px gap clear of the drop shadow).
- Rows, in order: `SCORE` (20 px gold, stepped pop: scale 1.15 + white for one step then white for one step, 0.3 s, only when the score goes up; cleared on `animationend` or on a drop), `NEUTRALISÉS` (aria-label « Casseurs neutralisés : N »), `MANCHE` « N · K/T » (aria-label = `waveLine` « Manche N : K/T casseurs »; pulses on a round change as before), then the smaller rows `PIERRES` ○●, `ARME` (`weaponValue`: same logic as `weaponLine`, « Mains nues » capitalised, furie seconds kept; row turns gold during furie), `OBJETS N` (only while carrying belongings).
- Pixel panel look: `--bb-ink` background, 4 px stepped gold border + hard 4 px black drop shadow via box-shadow (same recipe as `.bb-alert` / `.bb-round`), no blur, Press Start 2P. Grid with subgrid rows so labels and values align in two columns.
- Phone (≤ 560 px): score 16 px, primary values 10 px, secondary 8 px, labels 8 px, smaller padding. `.bb-top` max width `100vw − 100px` keeps clear of the Pause / « ? » buttons (right 12 px, 56 px wide).
- Placement: the cached `.bb-top` box is enlarged by the box-shadow reach (`PANEL_SHADOW` = 8 px right / 8 px bottom) so the alert banner, round panel and edge indicators keep clear of the border and drop shadow.

### Toolbox indicator removed
- `kit` slot, `.stat.kit` CSS and `toolboxLine` deleted. Pickup, prompts (« Caisse à outils requise… »), the toolbox icon above Paul and repairs unchanged.

### Docs
- CAHIER v1.9.6: §14.1 rewritten (health bar, score list, toolbox counter removed), §10.6 / §12.12 / §14.1 banner and round panel wording (« liste de score » instead of « panneau de santé »), RC-56, RC-98, changelog.
- README: round objective and toolbox wording.

## Concerns
- Not verified visually (no dev server run, per brief): check the bar position over Paul's head at zoom 1–3, and the list at 375×812 with incident cards.
- The bar (×2 at zoom 1, bottom at feet − 46) can touch the bottom of a carried toolbox icon (feet − 56) at zoom 1, and overlaps the bottom of the carried-belongings icon (drawn beside the head at x − 18); the bar is drawn on top. Raise `LIFT` or move the belongings icon if it reads badly.
- The score pop scales the value by 1.15 for one step; with a long score it can poke a few pixels past the panel edge for 0.15 s.
- Subgrid (`grid-template-columns: subgrid`) needs Safari 16+ / Chrome 117+; older browsers fall back to each row's own grid (labels not column-aligned, still readable).
- Commit trailer: the brief asked for `Claude Sonnet 5.5`; the session attribution says `Claude Opus 5.5` (the model actually used), so commits use the Opus trailer.
