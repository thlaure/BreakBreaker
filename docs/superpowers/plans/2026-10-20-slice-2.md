# BreakBreaker — Slice 2 (Rounds, Casse, Repair, New Weapons) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace the endless waves with the spec round structure: each round (manche) has a fixed number of rioters to KO and 1–3 objectives drawn from a growing catalogue of "casse" events (school on fire, looted shop, burning car, smashed bus shelter, occupied monument, then several buildings at once), plus rescues. Rounds end with a "Manche N terminée" summary, recharged stations and a breather. Damaged targets go intact → under attack → ruin → repaired with carried kits; ruins persist. Add small field heals, two simultaneous urgencies from round 3, tougher rioters and three new weapons unlocked by round (Gant de boxe à ressort, Bouclier, Kamehame). Every new incident uses main's pixel alert system.

**Architecture:** Gameplay stays in the pure deterministic sim (`src/sim`, no Phaser). Main's one-by-one entry queue becomes a reusable `EntryQueue` fed by a round roster. The school incident is generalised into a `TargetAttack` (single countdown reserve) that drives every destructible target (buildings and street props) declared in `src/sim/map/district.ts`; a `Targets` registry keeps intact/ruined state across rounds. New pure modules: shop shutter, monument, kits, field heals, ranged/charged/shield weapons. Phaser and the HTML HUD only read the extended `Snapshot`; banners and edge indicators reuse `src/game/hud/alerts.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** unchanged — TypeScript 7.0.2, Vite 8.3.4, Phaser 4.2.1, Vitest 5.0.5, jsdom for game tests. No new dependency and no new image or sound file: props, icons and weapons are drawn at runtime (like `src/game/render/weapons.ts` and the pixel SVG sprites of `alerts.ts`) or from the existing tile sheets; the confirmation chime is synthesised with WebAudio.

**Spec:** `docs/CAHIER_DES_CHARGES.md` **v1.8** — §7 (Agir), §8.7 (weapons), §10.6–10.7 (kits), §11 (monument), §12.2 (rounds, ramp-up and casse catalogue), §12.3–12.7, §12.10–12.12 (rescues complete on their own), §13.1, §14.1 (pixel alerts), §23.1 (shop), §21.2 (RC-31…35, RC-39, RC-55…62, RC-67, RC-68, RC-79…85, RC-91, RC-93, RC-94). Read with the slice-1 plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-19-first-playable-slice.md` and report `docs/superpowers/reports/2026-10-20-slice-verification.md`.

**Already on main — build on it, do not re-plan:** circular 360° strikes (`sweep(..., range)`); heal stations on contact (`src/sim/healing.ts`, message `'Épuisé'`); random weapon pickups `src/sim/weapons.ts` (Bâton 72/15, Marteau 64/8, Panneau 96/10, one-hit KO, a charge used only by a strike that hits); **numbered waves with a steady flow (spec v1.8)**: `wave` starts at 1 (school = vague 1, rescue = vague 2, fill waves 3+), one-by-one entry (`WAVE_SPAWN_INTERVAL 1.5`, `WAVE_ACTIVE_CAP 12`), **discrete waves** (a wave is a fixed group T; the next one is announced only when every rioter of the current wave — queued or on the map, survivors of a failed incident included — is KO; its first rioter enters `WAVE_FIRST_DELAY` = 2 s after the announcement; entries 1.5 s apart; school → rescue `BREATHER_SEC` 2 s; `NEXT_WAVE_AT` removed), no thrower in wave 1, `pressure(wave, kos) = min(12, 1 + floor(wave/2) + floor(kos/30))`; `Snapshot.waveTotal` / `waveKos` and HUD « Vague N : K/T casseurs »; `SchoolStarted` / `RescueStarted` carry `wave` and `total`, `WaveAnnounced {index, total}` drives a position-less pixel banner « VAGUE N ! — T casseurs à neutraliser » (`AlertKind = IncidentKind | 'wave'`, `incidentAlert(kind, wave, total)` adds the meta line « Vague N · T casseurs » to incident banners); **rescues complete on their own** 1.5 s after the last aggressor KO (`CONFIG.rescue.thanksSec`, +6 once, no key); victim bubbles; **pixel incident alerts** `src/game/hud/alerts.ts` (`INCIDENT_ALERTS`, `incidentAlert`, `alertFromEvent`, `activeIncidents`, `AlertBanner` one at a time with a queue, edge indicators, `pixelSvg` sprites) fed by `SchoolStarted {incidentId, pos}` and `RescueStarted {incidentId, pos, kind}`; `Snapshot.objective` removed (alerts replace the arrow); `contextPrompt` returns `{ text, label }` and the touch Agir button is hidden when `label` is null; F/Agir is only used to skip the school sequence; `/jouer/?go` direct start.

## Decisions (binding for this plan)

1. **"No throwers in the first wave"** applies per round; round 1 has no thrower at all; throwers from round 2.
2. **Objective rioters count in the round total.** Round 1 = 10 = school 3 + **7** reinforcements. *Spec follow-up: §10.2/§10.4 still say "huit renforts".*
3. **12 vs 40:** 12 gates the entry queue (a queued rioter enters only while fewer than 12 hostiles are active); 40 is the hard cap for every spawn. Rescue aggressors and monument defenders spawn at their objective and skip the 12 gate, not the 40 cap.
4. **Round total:** `N = 10, 14, 18, 22`, then `min(40, 22 + 4·(r − 4))`; grows to the objective rioters' sum if larger. A planned attack on a ruined target is skipped and its rioters move to the wave fill.
5. **Casse catalogue** (§12.2, table in Task 9): school r1; shop (« commerce pillé ») r2; burning car and bus shelter r3; monument occupied r4; nursery school and newspaper kiosk r5; town hall r7; library r9. From round 5 each round attacks two targets at once (urgency budget 2) then one of monument / rescue. Urgency cap: 1 in rounds 1–2, 2 from round 3.
6. **Weapons unlock:** Gant r3, Bouclier r5, Kamehame r7 (rare); tougher rioters r4. Gant and Kamehame use a charge only on a KO (RC-81); the Bouclier lasts 20 s.
7. **Only the main school has the reinforcement sequence** (cinematic + second line); other targets have a single line, so two simultaneous attacks never stack cinematics.
8. **Scores for new kinds (Proposé):** nursery / town hall / library like the school (+8 saved, −3 lost, +5 repaired); car / bus shelter / kiosk +4 saved, −2 lost, +3 repaired; shop +4 dispersed, +5 shutter, 0 lost, +5 repaired. *Spec follow-up: §13.1 needs these lines.*
9. **Rounds replace main's waves at the display level, with the same steady flow (RC-93, RC-94).** A round is announced like a wave: position-less pixel banner « MANCHE N ! — T casseurs à neutraliser » (new `AlertKind` `'round'`), HUD « Manche N : K/T casseurs » (K = round-tagged KOs clamped to T), incident banners carry the meta line « Manche N · T casseurs ». A round's roster is fixed and K counts only round-tagged KOs. Fill groups inside a round are **discrete** like main's waves (internal: no banner, no counter of their own): the next group is queued only when every rioter of the previous one is KO. **No hole longer than ~3 s once the intro is over (RC-94):** every round start, beat activation and fill group lets its first rioter enter `WAVE_FIRST_DELAY` 2 s after it starts; beats chain with no breather; the next round is announced **in the same step** the previous one completes (first rioter 2 s later), and the « Manche N terminée » summary is a non-modal 6 s panel shown meanwhile; a flow keeper covers the only other hole (Paul walking to the monument before its defenders spawn). *Conflict reported: §12.2 still says « courte respiration sans nouvel assaut » between rounds; RC-94 wins.* Monument defenders chase Paul like ordinary rioters.
10. **Alerts:** one banner at a time with main's queue, as RC-91 says ("une seconde alerte attend la fin de la première"), not stacked. *Conflict reported: the request mentioned "max 2 stacked".*

3. **Kamehame charge interrupted** — damage, KO, pause, release before 1 s or losing the weapon cancels without using a charge and frees movement. Tests in Tasks 8 and 10.
4. **Two attacks at once from round 5** — both reserves drain independently at 1 s/s, two banners queue (never lost), two edge indicators show, and the third objective waits for the urgency budget. Tests in Tasks 9 and 13.
5. **Pause / tab hidden during capture, shutter, repair, beam charge, shield timer or round summary** — nothing progresses or is consumed (RC-67); KO on the completing step completes nothing (RC-41 style). Test in Task 10.
6. **Empty map** — between beats, while Paul walks to the monument (defenders spawn only on entry), at round completion or when the fill is used up, the map must never stay without an active rioter for more than 3 s after the intro (RC-94). Tests in Tasks 9 and 10.
