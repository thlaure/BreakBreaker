## 7. Controls

| Action | Computer | Phone | Gamepad, if adopted |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | ZQSD, WASD or arrows | Left joystick | Left stick |
| Aim / orient | Mouse; falls back to the last movement direction | Movement direction, or drag from Throw back to aim | Right stick, falls back to movement |
| Strike (circular strike around Paul; **v1.9.15:** with the boxing glove, a straight punch toward the aim or facing; with the flashball, a shot toward the mouse or facing with aim assist; one strike per press, 0.45 s between two) | Space or left click (remapping to do) | Strike button | Main button |
| Charge the energy wave (**changed v1.9.14:** energy gauge at 50 or more, section 8.9) | **Changed (v1.9.23):** one press of C; the 0.7 s charge ends on its own (Paul stationary), without holding the key | Wave button (one press) | One press of a dedicated button |
| Trigger fury mode (**changed v1.9.14:** energy gauge full, section 8.8) | R (one press) | Fury button | Dedicated button |
| Throw back a carried stone | E or right click (remapping to do) | Throw-back button: tap = last aim, drag = aim | Secondary button |
| Pick up a stone from the ground | Automatic within range if stock is available | Automatic | Automatic |
| Pick up a weapon or the shield | Automatic when walking over it; a weapon replaces the held weapon, the shield is added to it (20 s protection, v1.9.16) | Automatic | Automatic |
| Pick up a toolbox or a stolen object | Automatic when walking over it | Automatic | Automatic |
| Heal at a station | Automatic when walking onto a charged station | Automatic | Automatic |
| Act: repair a ruin or extinguish a burning place with a toolbox (one press starts the action, 2 s without holding, v1.9.1); no action requires holding F (rescue and flag capture are automatic); **v1.9.19:** in front of a phone booth (door at most 40 px away), one press calls the fire brigade (red booth) or the police (blue booth), without holding (section 12.16) | F | Act button, named « Réparer » / « Éteindre » / « Appeler » (Repair / Extinguish / Call), hidden when no action is possible | Action button |
| Skip the school cutscene (round 1) | F, Space or click | Tap the screen | Action button |
| Run | Shift (hold) | Joystick pushed to the maximum | Button or stick press |
| Pause / stop | Esc / menu | Pause button | Menu |

The secondary click is not mandatory: a key must allow throwing back without depending on a context menu. On a phone, a simple press throws back toward the last aim direction; dragging allows a more precise direction. Aiming is used for throwing back and, **v1.9.15**, for the two weapons that strike in a straight line: the boxing glove (straight punch) and the flashball (shot) follow the mouse aim, failing that Paul's facing (with a slight aim assist for the flashball); with bare hands, the other weapons, the shield and in fury mode, the strike stays circular. No strike on a peaceful group can be triggered by a targeting aid. Act is no longer used to heal (section 12.6) or to finish a rescue (section 12.12).

Options (to do, none is delivered in the first slice): remapping, aim sensitivity, hold/toggle for striking, running and Act, disabling the aim drag if it gets in the way. The throw-back assist option only selects visible active rioters and does not pass through a group or an obstacle.

The touch pointers of the joystick and of the actions are independent. A cancellation, a menu or a loss of focus releases all buttons. The controls trigger no page zoom, no scrolling and no text selection.

## 14. Interface and accessibility

### 14.1 Interface

**Added (v1.9.9, project owner's request: « il faut qu’il y ait le nombre de points gagnés (en vert) et le nombre de points perdus (en rouge) qui apparaît puis disparaît depuis le bâtiment, le corps, etc. » — the number of points gained (in green) and points lost (in red) must appear then fade from the building, the body, etc.). Floating points.** Each score change displays, in the world, a small pixel text (Press Start 2P, one-pixel dark outline `#14121c`) at the spot where the points are gained or lost: « +N » in green (`#7bd88f`), « -N » in red (`#ff7b72`, hyphen of the pixel font, which has no typographic minus sign). Spot: the KO rioter (+1), the door of the place that was saved, destroyed, extinguished or repaired (the centre of the footprint for a car or the bus shelter), the victim (successful rescue, item returned), the centre of the monument zone (flag retaken, monument defended or lost), Paul for the round bonus. The text appears with a small zoom-in (80 ms), rises about 28 px in 1 s while slowing down and fades out during the last 40%; its size grows with the value (1 → 8 px, 2 to 4 → 10 px, 5 to 7 → 12 px, 8 and more → 14 px), doubled at zoom 1 like the bubbles. Several changes within the same frame (a ray that knocks out 8 rioters, items returned and end of the rescue, end of round) start one after the other (40 ms per rank, 8 ranks at most) and slightly offset sideways; at most 24 texts at once (the oldest disappears), each one dies on its own (purely visual: pause does not freeze them). `prefers-reduced-motion`: no zoom-in and no rise, appearance then fade in 0.6 s. No sound; the score list keeps its own jump. On the simulation side, each change applied by `Rules` (KO or deduplicated bonus/penalty, never after the end of the game, never a zero change) carries its position and becomes a `ScoreChanged { points, pos }` event; their sum over a game equals the final score.

**Changed (v1.9.7, project owner's request): centred menus.** The pause menu (Reprendre, Son, Aide, Crédits, Retour au site, Arrêter la partie — Resume, Sound, Help, Credits, Back to the site, Stop the game) is a narrow column centred on the screen, horizontally and vertically, title included; the « Retour au site » confirmation, the start screen, the help, the Controls modal and the summary are also centred. A panel taller than the screen (help, rules on a phone) starts at the top and scrolls, never cut off; the safe margins of notched screens are respected.

**Changed (v1.9.6, project owner's request).** **Paul's health above his head:** a pixel bar drawn in the world (about 28 × 5 px, dark outline `#14121c`, integer coordinates, sharp at the camera's integer zoom; doubled at zoom 1 like the victims' bubbles), green above 50%, yellow above 25%, red below, with a small discreet pixel heart on the left. It follows Paul's sprite, is drawn above him and his objects (the fury mode aura stays behind), flashes white with a 1 px shake when he loses health (no shake with `prefers-reduced-motion`) and disappears when he is KO. The HUD no longer has a visible health line; a visually hidden element (`role="progressbar"`, « Santé 85 sur 100 » — Health 85 out of 100) keeps the value for screen readers.

**Score list at the top left**, in pixel font (Press Start 2P), in the same pixel panel as the banners and cards (dark background, stepped gold border, hard 4 px drop shadow, no blur): one line per statistic, small muted label, large light value, in this order: « SCORE » (value of about 20 px, gold; it « jumps » for one frame, in steps, when the score increases), « NEUTRALISÉS » (the « Casseurs neutralisés » counter — Rioters neutralised — full text for screen readers), « MANCHE N · K/T » (rioters neutralised out of the round's roster, simply « MANCHE 1 » as long as no objective is set; full text « Manche N : K/T casseurs » for screen readers; the line blinks once, in steps, when the number changes), then in smaller type « PIERRES » (stones, three slots), « ARME » (weapon: « <name> (<hits>) » or « Mains nues » (bare hands), followed by « · Bouclier N s » while the shield protects Paul, v1.9.16, then by « · Mode furie N s » during fury mode), **added (v1.9.14):** « ÉNERGIE » (segmented pixel bar with a marker at 50%, which fills in steps and « jumps » when it gains; from 50 on « C · Onde » that blinks softly in blue, at 100 « R · Furie » that blinks in gold (**changed v1.9.23:** the key first, before « ONDE · C » / « FURIE · R ») — on a phone « ONDE ! » / « FURIE ! », the buttons bearing their name; during fury mode the gold bar drains over the 18 s with « EN FURIE »; « Énergie insuffisante » (not enough energy) in red for 1.2 s after a refused press; full text « Énergie N sur 100… » for screen readers) and « OBJETS N » (items) only when Paul is carrying stolen items to return. All these animations (blinking of the ÉNERGIE line and of the buttons included) are disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`. **Added (v1.9.14):** a thin pixel energy bar (cyan, lighter from 50, gold when full or during fury mode) is permanently drawn under Paul's health bar, the wave charge bar being displayed below it; on a phone, two round buttons « Onde » (one press, v1.9.23) and « Furie » (60 px) above Throw back, dimmed (dotted) while they are not available and glowing (blue, gold) when they are. On a phone (375 × 812) the list stays compact (score of about 16 px, lines of 8 to 10 px) and covers neither the Pause button, nor the Controls button, nor the touch controls. The old toolbox counter (« Caisse 0/1 ») is removed: the carried toolbox is visible above Paul and the « Caisse à outils requise… » (toolbox required) messages remain. Incident cards are displayed below the list, without covering it; banners, round panel and screen-edge indicators stay clear of the list (border and shadow included). Each attacked place has its own incident card with a mini health bar (« École 64 % », « en feu » (on fire) / « attaquée » (attacked)). Objectives and healing are indicated in the card and possibly on a minimap. Health of attacked places, local rescue deadline, capture and repair gauges, with no global countdown. Each incident card carries the name of its event, its distance and its state (« en feu / attaquée / renforts » (on fire / attacked / reinforcements) for a place, with its health « N % »; « en cours / interrompu / sécurisé » (in progress / interrupted / secured) for a rescue, with its deadline « N s » then « Rends les objets · N s » (Return the items); « à reprendre / en cours / zone contestée » (to retake / in progress / contested zone) for the flag, with « capture N/8 s »). A card disappears when its incident is settled (place saved or destroyed, rescue finished, flag retaken). The minimap remains an undelivered option.

Peaceful groups have a readable sign of neutrality and a distinct alert if a victim is in distress. A neutral icon must never be replaced by an enemy marker on a victim. The contact or assisted throw-back target cannot designate them. Projectiles must stand out from the ground, collectable stones, weapons on the ground, healing and scenery.

**Added (v1.9.21, project owner's request: « rien n’indique que F (Agir) peut réparer ou éteindre, ni où ; les alertes ne se voient pas assez » — nothing indicates that F (Act) can repair or extinguish, or where; the alerts are not visible enough). Contextual action in the world.** As soon as a fresh press of **F** (touch: Act) would do something, a pixel bubble floats above the place concerned (above its health bar, at the abscissa of its door): the « F » key drawn as a keyboard key then the verb, « Réparer » (Repair: ruin, carried toolbox, Paul in range, no rioter within 64 px), « Éteindre » (Extinguish: burning place, same conditions), « Appeler les pompiers » / « Appeler la police » (Call the fire brigade / the police: phone booth within calling range **and** a call that would be accepted). During the 2 s of repair the bubble displays « Réparation… » and an orange ring fills around the key (on a phone, a bar under the verb). Next to a ruin or a fire without a toolbox: « Il faut une caisse à outils » (A toolbox is needed; toolbox icon, no key) and a gold arrow around Paul points to the nearest toolbox on the ground. The bubble comes from the snapshot field `actionHint { kind: 'repair' | 'extinguish' | 'callFire' | 'callPolice' | 'needToolbox', target, at, toolbox }`, computed by the simulation with the same rule as the press (`agirChoice`: the nearest between the repair that would start and the booths in range, a tie going to the repair, then to the red one): it never promises what the press would not do; a call that would be refused no longer displays a prompt. The line under Paul remains only for screen readers when the bubble speaks; « Cabine hors service » (Booth out of service), « Abords non sécurisés » (Surroundings not secured) and the healing messages stay under Paul. On a phone, the Act button only appears for a possible action, bears the short verb (« Réparer », « Éteindre », « Appeler ») and pulses. **Repair guidance:** with Paul carrying a toolbox, each ruin bears a pulsing gold pixel wrench and, off screen, a dotted screen-edge indicator « RÉPARER » with arrow and distance; without a toolbox, as long as a ruin is waiting, each toolbox on the ground has its « OUTILS » (tools) indicator. **First ruin:** the first time a ruin exists in the game, a help line « Ramasse une caisse à outils, puis appuie sur F devant le bâtiment. » (Pick up a toolbox, then press F in front of the building.) (touch: « … puis touche Agir … ») is displayed for 7 s at the bottom centre, only once per game (HUD state, not saved). **Stronger alerts:** larger banner (title 20 px, text 10 px), short shake on entry for danger alerts, at least 3.5 s on screen (« LE CAÏD ARRIVE ! » (The Caïd is coming!), « DRAPEAU RÉCUPÉRÉ ! » (Flag recovered!) go from 2.6 to 3.5 s), alarm also on the Caïd's arrival, slight camera shake at each new danger (attack, fire, rescue, monument, Caïd); as long as a place is attacked it blinks red and a red ring pulses at its door, faster and faster as its health drops (900 ms at full health, 250 ms at 0); larger screen-edge indicators (10 px, 3 px border), their pulsing follows the same cadence; a brief red halo borders the screen at each new attack. With `prefers-reduced-motion`: no shake, no bubble bounce, no pulsing (rings, wrenches and flash fixed and dimmed), static dimmed halo.

**Incident alerts.** **Changed (v1.9.1):** fire is announced only when the bottle is thrown (« ÉCOLE EN FEU ! » (School on fire!), « VOITURE EN FEU ! » (Car on fire!), « ABRIBUS SACCAGÉ ! » (Bus shelter wrecked!) at ignition, line « Manche N »); the start of an attack displays no banner, its place already has its « attaquée » card and a screen-edge indicator in a yellow palette, alert icon, which switches to the fire palette, flame icon, once the place is on fire. When each incident spawns, a banner in the game's pixel style (pixel font, sharp borders, hard 4 px drop shadow, stepped animations) is displayed at the top centre, without covering the score list or the Controls button, readable down to 320 px wide: « ÉCOLE EN FEU ! » (orange/red palette, flame icon, « Rejoins l’école » — Go to the school), « VOITURE EN FEU ! » (same palette, « Chasse les casseurs » — Chase the rioters away), « ABRIBUS SACCAGÉ ! » (yellow palette, broken glass icon, « Arrête la casse » — Stop the destruction), « AGRESSION EN COURS ! » (Assault in progress; « Une personne est menacée » — A person is threatened) or « VOL EN COURS ! » (Theft in progress; « Une personne âgée est menacée » — An elderly person is threatened) in a yellow palette with an alert icon; shop and monument: table below. It carries a pixel arrow oriented from Paul toward the incident, updated continuously, and the distance in metres (one tile = 1 m). It disappears on its own after about six seconds; a second alert waits for the end of the first. An incident banner (displayed or pending) is removed as soon as its incident is no longer active (place saved or destroyed, rescue resolved), so as never to announce during the breather an emergency that is already over; the « MANCHE N ! » banners and the twist shield banner (section 8.9) follow the same queue. It is announced to screen readers (`role="alert"`) and is not interactive. Then, each active incident (place attacked or on fire, victim threatened, monument occupied or flag to retake) and each shield on the ground (section 8.9) whose target is off screen has a screen-edge indicator: icon, short label (« ÉCOLE », « VOITURE », « ABRIBUS », « BOUTIQUE », « AGRESSION », « VOL », « MONUMENT », « DRAPEAU », « CAÏD », « BOUCLIER »), distance and arrow, on a dark background (contrast ≥ 4.5:1), clear of the panel, the Controls button and the touch controls. All these animations are disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`. The system is generic (type, label, position): future incident events reuse it.

**Round announcement (v1.9).** Each round is announced by the same pixel banner (same queue, same duration, `role="alert"`): title « MANCHE N ! », subtitle « <headline incident> · T casseurs » (« École menacée », « Voiture menacée », « Abribus menacé », « Boutique menacée » (v1.9.4), « École maternelle menacée », « Kiosque menacé », « Mairie menacée », « Bibliothèque menacée » (v1.9.18), « Monument menacé » (v1.9.4), « Agression en cours » (v1.9.1) or « Vol en cours » for a rescue round whose victim is an elderly person being robbed (v1.9.12)), pixel horde icon in a red/orange palette, with no arrow or distance (a round has no position). Incident banners carry a second small line « Manche N · T casseurs ». **Changed (v1.9.7, v1.9.14):** only a twist shield is announced, with arrow and distance (section 8.9): « BOUCLIER DISPONIBLE ! » (Shield available!) / « Fonce le ramasser » (Run and grab it); an ordinary weapon has neither banner nor indicator, nor do fury mode and the energy wave (energy gauge, section 8.8). Activating fury mode displays a short banner (about 2 s) « MODE FURIE ! » / « Frappe, ils volent ! » (Strike, they fly!) with no arrow or distance. The layout stays compact and readable between 320 and 375 px wide.

**Round panel (v1.9).** During the breather, the « Manche N terminée » (Round N complete) panel (section 12.2) is displayed at the top centre, in the same pixel style, under the banner if there is one and under the score list when the screen is too narrow; it never covers Paul (centre of the screen) or the score list, is not interactive (`role="status"`) and disappears when play resumes.

Display the states: enemy preparation, stone in flight, recoverable stone, full stock, weapon on the ground, broken weapon, healing charged or exhausted, health at maximum, contested circle, objective resolved, end of round. Announcements must not hide an impact zone or an escape route. At most two urgent alerts are detailed; a selection displays the route or direction toward the chosen incident.

### 14.2 Accessibility

Remapping; large touch actions; hold or toggle; aim options; contrast; HUD size; sounds doubled by visual cues; keyboard focus; automatic pause. No camera control to learn in V1. **Reduced motion (v1.9.17):** with `prefers-reduced-motion`, no screen shake, impact freezes halved and KO falls shorter without spin, jump or oscillation (section 12.15).

Rescue alerts have text/icon in addition to a sound and their colour. Sprites may be drawn at low resolution, but the rules text and the HUD must remain readable at a suitable CSS size. A bitmap font that is too small is not imposed. Red/green colours are complemented by shapes, icons or text.

Landscape recommended, portrait supported with an adapted play area and buttons. Do not simply shrink the whole landscape screen into a thumbnail. The phone's system areas stay clear.

The [Game Accessibility Guidelines](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/basic/) and the [full list](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/) serve as a reference, without a claim of full certification.

### 14.3 Modes

Normal (the only delivered mode): health, healing, hordes and reference trajectories. Discovery (to do): reduced damage, longer throw preparation and stronger aim assist, with a separate record. No mode imposes a global time limit.

Visual quality never changes the size of hitboxes, the speed of stones, the pressure, the stock or the score. A low quality keeps all useful alerts.
