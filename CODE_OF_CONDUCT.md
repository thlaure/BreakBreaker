# Code of Conduct

We want BreakBreaker to be a friendly place to build and play. Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests and discussions, agrees to:

- be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith;
- welcome people of every background and experience level;
- give and accept feedback graciously;
- not harass, insult, discriminate against or attack anyone, and not post other people's private information.

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported privately to the maintainer through the contact options on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block contributors who break these rules.

This text is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
